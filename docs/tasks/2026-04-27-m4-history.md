# Task: Conversation History (Milestone 4, Session 9)

**Date:** 2026-04-27
**Phase:** Phase 1 — Milestone 4
**Session goal:** Upgrade Orchestrator to persist conversation history (last 10 turns) so follow-up questions work without losing context.
**Notion task ID:** (next card after 34e7600e54c781da8290e0c96c87ea64)

## What We're Building
A stateful `Orchestrator` that keeps a rolling 10-turn history and passes it with every `ollama.chat` call.
The `Overlay` owns one shared `Orchestrator` instance, passes it into `AskThread`, and clears history when the overlay is hidden.

## Approach
1. Add `__init__` to `Orchestrator` with `self._history: list[dict] = []`
2. Modify `ask()` to build `[system, *history, user]`, append both turns after response, trim to 10 pairs (20 messages)
3. Add `clear_history()` method on `Orchestrator`
4. Modify `AskThread` to accept an existing `Orchestrator` instead of creating one
5. In `Overlay.__init__`, create `self._orchestrator = Orchestrator()`
6. Pass `self._orchestrator` to `AskThread` calls
7. Call `self._orchestrator.clear_history()` in `toggle()` when hiding and on Escape-to-close
